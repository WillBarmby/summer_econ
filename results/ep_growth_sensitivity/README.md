# E&P Deterministic-Growth Sensitivity

**Question:** are the E&P learning results driven by the original positive
deterministic growth rate rather than the expectations formulation?

The experiment changes gross trend growth from `exp(0.0053)` to one while
holding shocks and all other experiment settings fixed. Values below are the
maximum absolute zero-growth-minus-original-growth differences in the median
learning response.

| Specification | Output | Consumption | Investment | Hours |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| E&P EE | 0.0093 | 0.0268 | 0.1555 | 0.0299 |
| E&P IH | 0.0108 | 0.0384 | 0.2311 | 0.0447 |

Trend growth changes magnitudes, particularly investment, but does not explain
the much larger benchmark IH wedge. Primary files:
`ep_growth_sensitivity.mat` and `ep_growth_sensitivity_panels.pdf`.
