# Cross-Model Technology-Shock Comparison

**Question:** under identical technology innovations and settings, how do the
within-model learning-minus-RE wedges compare across E&P RBC and the
balanced-growth NK model?

| Specification | Output | Consumption | Investment | Hours | Completed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| E&P EE | 0.0053 | 0.0028 | 0.0290 | 0.0081 | 100/100 |
| E&P IH | 0.2095 | 0.1055 | 1.1316 | 0.3151 | 100/100 |
| NK EE | 0.0015 | 0.0030 | 0.0147 | 0.0017 | 100/100 |

The one-step EE specifications remain close to their own RE benchmarks; IH is
much farther away. The five-path overlay combines structural and expectations
differences, so `learning_wedges.pdf` is the controlled comparison. The complete
artifact is `cross_model_comparison.mat`.
