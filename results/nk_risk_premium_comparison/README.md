# NK Risk-Premium RE/EE Comparison

**Question:** does one-step EE learning materially change real and nominal NK
responses to an IID one-percentage-point risk-premium innovation?

At gain `0.002`, all 100 draws complete. Maximum absolute median
learning-minus-RE wedges are:

| Output | Consumption | Investment | Hours | Inflation | Nominal rate |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.0069 | 0.0018 | 0.0279 | 0.0103 | 0.0024 | 0.0035 |

The benchmark effects are modest and become larger in the gain sensitivity.
The shock scale is illustrative rather than empirically estimated. Primary
files: `nk_risk_premium_comparison.mat` and `nk_risk_premium_panels.pdf`.
